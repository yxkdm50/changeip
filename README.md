## The DDNS Thing, Explained Simply

Here's the deal with Dynamic DNS if you haven't run into it before. Your home internet connection doesn't have a permanent address — your ISP hands you a new IP every so often, like a surprise no one asked for. For most people, totally fine. But if you're running anything — a Plex server, a NAS, a home security system, a small business site — on your own hardware, you need people (or your own devices) to be able to find that machine consistently.

Dynamic DNS is the bridge. It keeps your domain name pointing at wherever your IP currently is, updating in real time. ChangeiP has been doing this longer than most of their competitors have existed, and their update system is lightweight and reliable.

The free tier is genuinely free — no credit card, no trial period that quietly becomes a subscription. You pick one of their pre-selected domain names (think subdomain-style hostnames), and you're off. For a homelab or a side project, it works.
